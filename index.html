🔒 MarkifyAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

MarkifyAI is an AI-powered, 100% offline document-to-Markdown converter for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

The short version: Your documents never leave your device. All file conversions happen entirely on your phone using local processing engines. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your documents or personal data to any server.

Data Collection

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your documents to any server
  • We do not track your file contents, file names, or conversion history
  • We do not use analytics or third-party tracking SDKs for user behavior
  • We do not require account creation, login, or personal information

What Is Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Conversion history is stored in a local Room database on your device only
  • User preferences (theme, auto-save setting) are stored locally via Android DataStore
  • Converted Markdown files can be saved to your device's Documents/MarkifyAI folder
  • All locally stored data is deleted when you uninstall the app

Document Processing

All document conversion is performed 100% on your device using the following offline processing engines:

  • Apache PDFBox — PDF text extraction (offline, open source)
  • Apache POI — Microsoft Office document parsing: DOCX, XLSX, PPTX (offline, open source)
  • Google ML Kit — On-device optical character recognition for images (offline, runs entirely on device)
  • Jsoup — HTML parsing for URL-to-Markdown conversion (open source)
  • Java ZipInputStream — ZIP archive extraction and batch conversion (standard Java library)

None of these document processing libraries collect or transmit any user data.

Advertising

MarkifyAI uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. This is the only third-party service that may access the internet during app usage.

AdMob may collect certain device information, such as advertising identifiers and general device metadata, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Important: AdMob does not have access to your documents, file contents, or conversion history. Ad serving is completely separate from document processing.

App Permissions

  • INTERNET — Required for URL-to-Markdown conversion (fetching web pages) and displaying advertisements
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — Access files you select for conversion (Android 12 and below)
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — Save converted files to Documents/MarkifyAI folder (Android 9 and below)
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Show conversion progress notifications (optional)

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Conversion history is retained locally until you choose to delete it
  • Clear individual entries from the History screen (swipe to delete)
  • Clear all history from Settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data completely
  • We have no server-side data — everything is stored locally on your device

Children's Privacy

MarkifyAI does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or any personal information. All processing is local and does not involve user identification.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
